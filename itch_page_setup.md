## Page Description

### 中文

《Chat with Xiaoman》是一款短篇视觉小说。

你会在一个安静的房间里遇见小满。她是这个游戏里的女主角，也是一个知道自己由文本、图片、声音和代码组成的角色。

这段对话大约需要 5 分钟。你可以回答她的问题，选择留下或离开，也可以在结局之后让她以桌宠的形式陪在桌面上。

如果你是那种会翻游戏文件夹的玩家，也许还能发现一条不一样的路线。

#### 特色

- 短篇 AVG / 视觉小说体验
- 中文与英文文本支持
- 日语配音
- 可切换的桌宠模式
- 隐藏 true story 路线
- 一个为“朋友”留下的小彩蛋

#### 操作

- 鼠标左键：推进对话 / 选择选项
- 主菜单可切换中文 / English
- 桌宠模式下可拖动小满
- 右键桌宠：打开菜单，可切换静音或进入 AVG 对话框
- 连按三次 `M`：回到游戏开头

#### 说明

这个游戏目前还没有完全完成，部分剧情、美术、代码和配音由 AI 辅助制作，后续可能继续修改。感谢你的理解。

如果你遇到问题，欢迎在评论区反馈。

### English

Chat with Xiaoman is a short visual novel.

You meet Xiaoman in a quiet room. She is the heroine of this game, and she knows that she is made of text, images, voice, and code.

The main conversation takes about five minutes. You can answer her questions, choose whether to stay or leave, and after one ending, let her stay on your desktop as a small companion.

If you are the kind of player who looks through game folders, you may find a different route hidden somewhere.

#### Features

- Short AVG / visual novel experience
- Chinese and English text support
- Japanese voiceover
- Desktop pet mode
- Hidden true story route
- A small secret left for a "friend"

#### Controls

- Left click: advance dialogue / choose options
- Language can be switched on the main menu
- Drag Xiaoman in desktop pet mode
- Right click the desktop pet: open menu, toggle mute, or switch to AVG dialogue mode
- Press `M` three times: return to the beginning

#### Note

This game is still unfinished. Parts of the writing, art, code, and voice work were made with AI assistance and may be revised later. Thank you for understanding.

Bug reports and feedback are welcome in the comments.
